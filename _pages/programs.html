Programs & Services

We currently run three programs for Jewish families in Crown Heights and the surrounding community. Each one is designed to respond quickly and to treat every family with privacy and respect.

Family Support Emergency Assistance Food Assistance

Family Support

Some families are not facing a single emergency, but a long stretch of strain: a large household on one income, a parent recovering from illness, a mother home with a newborn, or a family that has just arrived in Crown Heights and is still finding its footing. Our Family Support program helps these households over a period of time, so that a difficult season does not become a crisis.

Support may include

  • Help with essential household costs for families under ongoing financial pressure
  • Assistance for families after the birth of a baby, including meals and essentials
  • Help with children's needs, such as clothing and school supplies
  • Support for families coping with serious illness or bereavement
  • Guidance and connection to other community resources and services

Emergency Assistance

Crises do not wait. When a family suddenly cannot pay the rent, has its electricity or gas about to be shut off, or faces an urgent expense it cannot cover, our Emergency Assistance program provides one-time financial help so the family can get through the immediate danger.

Examples of emergencies we help with

  • Overdue rent to help prevent eviction
  • Utility bills facing shut-off (electricity, gas, heat)
  • Urgent medical, dental or prescription costs not covered by insurance
  • Sudden loss of income due to job loss, illness or a death in the family
  • Losses caused by a fire, flood or other unexpected event in the home

Wherever possible, emergency payments are made directly to the landlord, utility company or provider, so that every dollar goes exactly where it is needed.

Food Assistance

No family should go hungry, and no family should have to go without for Shabbos or Yom Tov. Our Food Assistance program helps families put kosher food on the table, with special attention to the weeks before the Jewish holidays, when household costs rise sharply.

Food assistance may include

  • Kosher groceries or grocery store vouchers for families in need
  • Help with food for Shabbos
  • Holiday assistance before Pesach, Rosh Hashanah, Sukkos and other Yamim Tovim
  • Meals for families after a birth, during illness, or while sitting shiva

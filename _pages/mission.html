Our Mission

To provide financial assistance to Jewish families in need, with a particular focus on New York, and to strengthen Jewish life in the spirit of the Chabad Lubavitch movement.

Statement of purpose

The purpose of this Corporation is to provide financial assistance to people in the broader Jewish community in the United States, with a particular focus on the Jewish community in New York, and to promote the Jewish religious principles of the Chabad Lubavitch movement through religious activities, as approved by the Corporation’s Board of Directors. The Corporation may engage in any other activities permissible under New York State law.

Two commitments

Financial assistance for families

Our first commitment is material: helping Jewish families who are struggling to meet basic needs. We do this through three programs: Family Support, Emergency Assistance and Food Assistance. Our primary focus is Crown Heights, Brooklyn, where our volunteers live and where we know the needs firsthand.

Strengthening Jewish life

Our second commitment is spiritual. Guided by the teachings of the Chabad Lubavitch movement, we support religious activities approved by our Board of Directors, such as helping families celebrate Shabbos and the Jewish holidays with dignity and joy, and encouraging the mitzvos of tzedakah (charity) and chessed (kindness).

The values behind our work

  • Tzedakah. In Jewish tradition, charity is not a favor but an obligation of justice. We see helping a family as our responsibility, not their burden.
  • Dignity. The highest form of giving preserves the dignity of the person receiving it. We work discreetly and keep the names of the families we help private.
  • Community. We are neighbors helping neighbors. Strong families make a strong community.
  • Joy in Jewish life. No family should have to go without the basics for Shabbos and Yom Tov.
  • Transparency. Donors deserve to know their gifts are used responsibly. As a registered 501(c)(3) charity, we are accountable to our Board, our donors and the public.

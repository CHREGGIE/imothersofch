# Brand style guide

**Israeli Mothers of Crown Heights Incorporated**

This guide covers the colors and type used on imothersofch.org. Use it for printed materials, social media, donation receipts, and as a brief for any designer you hire.

The CMYK values are calculated conversions. For anything printed in quantity, ask the printer for a proof, or have a designer match the colors to Pantone.

### Using the colors

- **Mostly light, with a little plum.** Pages are white and cream. Plum appears in the logo, buttons, links and the footer.
- **Gold is an accent only.** Use Champagne Gold for small details such as the candle flames and thin rules, never for large areas or body text.
- **Readability.** Ink, Deep Plum and Plum text all pass WCAG AA contrast on white and Cream. On Plum backgrounds, use white, On-Plum Text or Champagne Gold.

## Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weights | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headings and name | **Cormorant Garamond** | 500, 600, 500 italic | Google Fonts (free) |
| Body text | **Mulish** | 400, 600, 700 | Google Fonts (free) |
| Hebrew (בס״ד) | **Frank Ruhl Libre** | 500 | Google Fonts (free) |

- Headings are set in Cormorant Garamond in sentence case, never all capitals.
- Italic Maroon Rose is the accent style, as in "*of Crown Heights*".

## Name and logo

- **Header lockup:** "Israeli Mothers" on the first line (Cormorant Garamond 600, with "Israeli" in Maroon Rose and "Mothers" in Deep Plum), and "*of Crown Heights* Inc." smaller on the second line (italic, Maroon Rose, with "Inc." in Mulish, Muted).
- **Full legal name:** use "Israeli Mothers of Crown Heights Incorporated" on receipts, legal documents and the website footer.
- **Logo mark:** three Shabbos candles (two tall candles side by side, with a smaller candle beside them) on a Plum circle, with Champagne Gold flames and white candles. The file is at `assets/favicon.svg`.

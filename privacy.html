Privacy Policy

How Israeli Mothers of Crown Heights Incorporated handles information on imothersofch.org.

Information we collect

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics, and it does not ask you to create an account. We only receive personal information when you choose to send it to us, for example by email, by phone, or by mailing a donation.

How we use your information

We use the information you share with us only to respond to you, to provide assistance you have requested, to process and acknowledge donations, and to keep the records required of a registered charity.

Families we help

Information about families who ask us for help is treated as strictly confidential. It is shared only with the members of our team who need it to provide assistance, and with payees (such as a landlord or utility) when we make a payment on a family's behalf. We never publish the names of the families we help.

Donors

We do not sell, rent or trade donor information. We may contact you to acknowledge your gift or to share updates about our work; you can ask us to stop at any time.

External links

This site may link to outside websites, such as Google Maps. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@imothersofch.org or by mail to Israeli Mothers of Crown Heights Incorporated, 49 E 43 St, Brooklyn, NY 11203.
